Transparent numerical conventions

Modern interface. Explicit physics.

Every field retains its published physical definition. The interface exposes the interpolation, weighting, and regrouping choices instead of hiding them behind a generic “opacity” label.

STA

Atomic opacity model

How the opacities were calculated

The underlying monochromatic opacities were calculated with STAR (STA-Revised), an atomic code based on the Super-Transition-Array method for plasmas in local thermodynamic equilibrium. The STA approach statistically represents the immense number of atomic configurations and transitions in a tractable form; GLOW then integrates those spectra into 1024 energy groups and builds the Planck- and Rosseland-weighted quantities served here.

Method reference Krief, Feigel & Gazit (2016) “Solar Opacity Calculations Using the Super-Transition-Array Method” · ApJ 821, 45
P

Planck absorption

kplanck uses a Planck-weighted arithmetic mean of true absorption.

S

Planck scattering

kplanck_scattering uses the same Planck arithmetic weighting for scattering.

R

Rosseland total

krosseland is the harmonic Rosseland mean of absorption plus scattering.

A

Transport absorption

krosseland_absorption retains the total opacity in its transport denominator.

i

Published 1024-group values are treated as piecewise constant inside each native group. Custom boundaries integrate the physical weight only over the overlapping interval. Extrapolation outside the published temperature, density, and energy ranges is disabled.
