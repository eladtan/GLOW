Custom opacity queries

Choose native coordinates or enter physical temperature and density values. Custom values are interpolated linearly in log(T), log(rho), and log(kappa). Energy regrouping uses the weighting associated with the selected opacity field.

Custom energy edges that cut through a native group treat that native opacity as constant within the group while integrating the physical weight only over the overlapping energy interval.

Numerical conventions

kplanck and kplanck_scattering are regrouped with Planck-weighted arithmetic means. krosseland uses a Rosseland harmonic mean. krosseland_absorption retains its original Rosseland flux-weighted form with the total Rosseland opacity in the transport denominator. Published 1024-group values are treated as piecewise constant inside each native group. Extrapolation outside the published temperature, density, and energy ranges is disabled.
